HoloVoice neural voices: target voice references
=================================================

Each file is a short recording of one real speaker from a public speech dataset. The sidecar uses the first
--prompt-sec seconds (default 6) after the leading silence, brought to a fixed level. voices.json names
them for the app (the timbre "styles" of the neural female / male voices).

All files: 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit PCM. Changes to every file: resampled to 22.05 kHz, silence trimmed,
clips concatenated, cut to at most 14 s, peak-normalized.

Chosen from 90 candidates: LJSpeech (public domain), LibriTTS-R, Hi-Fi TTS and VCTK (CC BY 4.0). Each
candidate converted HoloVoice's Russian test recordings (one male and one female speaker) through the
streaming sidecar. The scores were UTMOS, speaker similarity to the reference (WavLM-ECAPA), Whisper CER and
pitch. The three voices per gender were picked to sound clearly different (median output pitch about 205 / 175 / 215 Hz
female, 105 / 80 / 125 Hz male).
